Privacy policy

Road to Nordkapp is made by Nordic Digital Apps AS (org. no. [ORGANISASJONSNUMMER], [ADRESSE], Norway). We are the controller of the information described here.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

In short

The game has no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. The only information sent from the game is what is needed to confirm the purchase of Full Roadtrip.

Stored on your device

Settings, progress, stars, coins and whether you have bought Full Roadtrip are stored only on your device. We have no access to them. They are deleted when you uninstall the game (or kept in your device's own backup, if you use one).

In-game purchase

Full Roadtrip is bought through the store you downloaded the game from. Payment is handled by the store, and we never see your payment details.

To confirm and restore the purchase we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA), which processes the information on our behalf. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous ID created by the game,
  • the purchase history linked to that ID: which product, when, country and currency, and the store receipt,
  • technical information needed to respond, for example the app version and the IP address of the request.

The anonymous ID is not linked to your name, your email or your account with the store.

The information is used to give you access to what you bought and for aggregated sales statistics. The legal basis is our contract with you for the purchase (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

Transfers to the USA rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent basis used by RevenueCat.

How long

Purchase history is kept as long as needed to restore your purchase and as long as accounting rules require.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction or deletion, and you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (datatilsynet.no) or the data protection authority where you live.

Because the game has no account, we may need your store receipt (the order number) to find your purchase. Write to [KONTAKT-E-POST].

Children

The game does not collect information about who you are, whatever your age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated.
