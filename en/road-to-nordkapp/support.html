Support

Questions about Road to Nordkapp or buying Full Roadtrip? Here are answers to the most common ones, and how to contact us.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Contact

Write to [KONTAKT-E-POST]. If it is about the purchase, include your user ID (Settings → About the game) and the order number from the store's receipt. Please also tell us which phone or tablet you use.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Make sure you are signed in to the store with the same account you used to buy Full Roadtrip. Open the game and go to Settings → Journey → Restore purchases. The button is also on the Full Roadtrip card in Route. Once the purchase is found, stages 5–11 are open again.

The game cannot find my purchase. What do I do?

Check that your device is online and that you are signed in to the store with the same account you used to buy. If that does not help, write to [KONTAKT-E-POST] with your user ID and the order number from the receipt, and we will help you.

Where do I find my user ID?

Go to Settings → About the game. Your User ID is shown there, and the “Copy user ID” button copies it so you can paste it into your email. The ID is random and created by the game. It is not linked to your name, your email or your account with the store.

I have a new phone or have reinstalled the game

The purchase follows your account with the store: use Restore purchases as described above. Progress and settings are stored only on the device, and we cannot move them. They can only come back from your device's own backup. A new installation gets a new user ID.

What do I get with Full Roadtrip?

Stages 5–11, from Trondheim to Nordkapp. One purchase, no subscription and no ads. Stages 1–4, from Oslo to Trondheim, are free.

How do I get a refund?

You pay through the store you downloaded the game from, and the store handles refunds. Please contact the store. We cannot see or change the payment.

How do I delete my data?

What is on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. If you also want us to delete the purchase history held by RevenueCat, write to [KONTAKT-E-POST] with your user ID or the order number from the receipt. Read more in the privacy policy.
