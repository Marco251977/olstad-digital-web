Saldo Pocket

Delete account and data

Your Saldo Pocket data is stored on your phone, so you delete it yourself in the app. No request is needed.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Delete everything in the app

  1. Open Saldo Pocket.
  2. Go to Me.
  3. Tap Delete all data on this phone.
  4. Tap Delete everything on this phone.

This deletes everything the app has on your phone: accounts, entries, recurring items, goals, categories, vendors, settings, your sign-in, saved pictures and files, the widget's data, the device ID and your choice of language and currency. The app then starts again as new.

The count of free scans and file imports stays – only numbers, nothing about you or your data – both on the phone and on our server, so the free limit still applies.

It cannot be undone. If you want a copy, export your data first under Me → Export data. It is free.

Uninstalling the app

Uninstalling Saldo Pocket also deletes all its data from your phone.

Exception: the count of free scans and file imports (only numbers) may stay in the phone's keychain (iPhone) or in Android's backup, so the free limit still applies after a new installation. If backup is turned on on your phone, a copy of the app's data stays with Apple or Google until you delete it there.

What is left on our server

If you have used AI reading, our server has a count of readings per device and day, stored as a one-way code and without any content. It is deleted automatically after 30 days and cannot be linked to you.

Free AI scans are also counted per month with a hashed device ID (a one-way code, never the ID itself). The hashed device ID is used only to count free AI scans, not for tracking, and the count is deleted automatically after 13 months.

Your purchase and purchase history stay with the App Store or Google Play and RevenueCat, so you can restore Pro. The services we use have their own logs, which are deleted under their terms.

Deleting your account

If you use the app without signing in, your data is only on your phone. It is deleted when you delete all data in the app, or when you remove the app from your phone.

If you are signed in, the data in the cloud is also deleted when you delete your account in the app: the account, everything synced to it, and everything on the phone you delete from. If you signed in with Facebook, what we got from Facebook is deleted too. You can also remove Saldo Pocket in your Facebook settings. What stays is described above.

If you only delete the data on your phone, you are signed out, but your account and your data in the cloud stay. Data on your other devices is not deleted automatically; delete it in the app there. Deleting does not cancel a subscription; you do that in the App Store or Google Play.

If you no longer have the app

Write to kontakt@nordicdigitalapp.no with the subject «Delete Saldo Pocket data». We reply within 30 days. If you used the app without signing in, we usually have nothing stored about you, and we confirm that. If you had an account, give the e-mail address you signed in with, and we delete the account and the data in the cloud and confirm it.
