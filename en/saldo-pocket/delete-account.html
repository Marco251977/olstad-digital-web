Saldo Pocket

Delete account and data

Your Saldo Pocket data is stored on your phone, so you delete it yourself in the app. No request is needed.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Delete everything in the app

  1. Open Saldo Pocket.
  2. Go to Me.
  3. Tap Delete all data.
  4. Tap Delete everything.

This deletes everything the app keeps on your phone: accounts, entries, recurring items, goals, categories, vendors, settings, saved pictures and files, the device ID, and your language and currency choices. The app then starts again as new.

It cannot be undone. If you want a copy, export your data first under Me → Export data. It is free.

Uninstalling the app

Uninstalling Saldo Pocket also deletes all its data from your phone.

What is left on our server

If you have used AI reading, our server keeps a count of readings per device ID and day, without any content. It is deleted automatically after 30 days. The device ID is random and is deleted from your phone with everything else, so the count cannot be linked to you.

Deleting your account

If you use the app without signing in, your data is only on your phone. It is deleted when you delete all data in the app, or when you remove the app from your phone.

If you are signed in, your data in the cloud is also deleted when you delete your account in the app: the account, everything synced to it, and everything on the phone you delete from. If you signed in with Facebook, what we received from Facebook is deleted too. You can also remove Saldo Pocket in your Facebook settings.

If you only delete the data on your phone, you are signed out, but your account and your data in the cloud stay. Data on your other devices is not deleted automatically; delete it in the app there. Deleting does not cancel a subscription; you do that in the App Store or Google Play.

If you no longer have the app

Write to kontakt@nordicdigitalapp.no with the subject “Delete Saldo Pocket data”. We answer within 30 days. Because there is no account, we usually have nothing stored about you, and we will confirm that.
