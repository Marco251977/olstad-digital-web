What the app is

Saldo Pocket gives you an overview of your money: accounts, entries, budget and goals. The figures are based on what you enter and import. They are an overview, not financial advice, so check important decisions with your bank.

Account and sync

You can use the app without an account. If you sign in and have Pro, your data is synced to your account so you can use it on several devices. Keep your sign-in safe; anyone who can sign in to your account can see the synced data.
