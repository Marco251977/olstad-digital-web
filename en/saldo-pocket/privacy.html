Saldo Pocket

Privacy policy

This policy explains what Saldo Pocket stores, what it sends and to whom, and what you can do about it. The short version: your budget stays on your phone.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

  • Privacy policy
  • Terms of use
  • Delete account and data
  • Support

Who is responsible

Olstad Digital (org. no. 938 090 432, Norway) is responsible for the processing described here. Contact us at kontakt@nordicdigitalapp.no.

In short

  • Your budget is stored on your phone. Without an account, we cannot see it.
  • An account is optional (Apple, Google, Facebook or e-mail). With an account and Pro, your budget is synced to our server in the EU so you can use it on several devices.
  • There is no bank connection.
  • CSV and Excel files from your bank are read only on your phone. The file itself is never stored, copied or sent anywhere.
  • Nothing from your budget leaves the phone unless you sync with Pro or choose AI reading of a statement, receipt or bill.
  • Crash reports contain no personal data.
  • You can export everything and delete everything in the app, for free.

Stored on your phone

The app stores this in its own storage on your phone. It is not sent to us, and it is deleted when you delete all data in the app or uninstall it:

  • The accounts you create (names you choose, balance and currency; not bank accounts).
  • Your entries: date, amount, currency, category, vendor and an optional note.
  • Recurring items, savings goals, your own categories, and the category remembered for each vendor.
  • Settings: payday, monthly budget, your language and currency, and whether you allow AI reading.
  • A random device ID, used only to count AI readings (see below).
  • Pictures you choose to keep after an import.

KID numbers are never stored, and neither is the full account number. The app may store the last four digits of the account number to recognize the account.

Importing CSV and Excel files

When you import a CSV or Excel file from your bank, the file is read only on your phone. The file itself is never stored, copied or sent anywhere – not to a server and not to AI.

From the file, the app stores only the date (and the booking date when the file has one), description, amount, vendor, the bank’s balance for reconciling the account, and the last four digits of the account number, to recognize the account. KID numbers, other references and the file’s other columns are not stored.

Entries the bank has not booked yet are stored as ordinary entries and counted straight away. If such an entry is missing from a later import from your bank, the app asks whether you want to remove it. It is never removed by itself.

The app remembers how the columns in the file are laid out, without their content, so the next import is quicker. When you are signed in with Pro, this is synced with your other settings.

AI reading is used only for pictures and PDFs, and only when you have said yes.

AI reading of transaction lists, receipts and bills (optional)

When you import a screenshot or photo of a transaction list from your bank, or scan a receipt or bill, you can let AI read it. The app asks for your permission first, and you can turn it off at any time on the import screen. Without permission, everything is read on the phone and nothing is sent.

With permission, the picture (or the text read from it), the currency, your country, today’s date, the app’s list of categories and the device ID are sent through our server at Supabase (Ireland) to Anthropic, which reads the rows with its AI model (Claude) and sends them back. Neither we nor Supabase store the picture, the text or the rows. Anthropic does not use data sent through its API to train its models, and deletes it after a limited time under its own terms.

To keep the service within its limits (a limited number of readings per device), our server stores the device ID, the date and the number of readings, and nothing else. These counts are deleted automatically after 30 days.

For the free version’s limit on AI scans, our server counts scans per month with a hashed device ID – a one-way code made from the phone’s ID with a secret key, never the ID itself. The hashed device ID is used only to count free AI scans, not for tracking. Only the code, the month and the number are stored, and the count is deleted automatically after 13 months.

Scanning receipts and bills

When you scan a receipt or bill, the picture is read on your phone, or by AI if you have said yes (see above). The picture is deleted as soon as it has been read, even if you cancel or something goes wrong. From the picture, only the store, date, amount and category are stored. KID numbers and account numbers are never stored.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or an error occurs, a report is sent to Sentry (in the EU): what went wrong and where in the app, the app version, and the phone model and system version. Collection of personal data, such as IP address or user identity, is turned off. Reports are kept for at most 90 days and are used only to fix errors.

App updates

The app checks for updates from Expo, the service that delivers them. As with any connection to the internet, Expo sees the phone’s IP address. No other data is sent.

Buying Pro

Pro is bought in the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment under their own privacy policies; we never see your card details. RevenueCat keeps track of whether you have Pro, with an anonymous ID the app makes, or your account ID when you are signed in, so Pro follows your account.

Optional account and sync (Pro)

You can sign in with Apple, Google, Facebook (Meta) or e-mail. Sign-in is handled by Supabase on servers in Ireland. We then store your e-mail address, an account ID, your name if the provider shares it, your app language and, on test versions, a test flag. With Apple you can hide your e-mail address; we then only see Apple’s relay address.

With Pro, your budget is synced to your account: accounts, entries, recurring items, budget, savings goals, your own categories, remembered vendors and the settings that follow you (payday, salary, budget). Each change is stored with when it was made and on which device. The data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and only your account can read it.

Sign-in codes and links are sent by e-mail through Resend, with your address and language. To send each e-mail once, the server keeps only the message ID, type and status for 7 days, never the address.

You can sign out at any time; the data stays on the phone. “Delete account” removes the account and all data stored with it on the server.

Widget and reminders

The widget shows numbers from the data on your phone, and reminders for recurring items are set up on your phone. Nothing is sent for either.

Who we share data with

  • Supabase (Ireland, EU): sign-in, sync for Pro, and the server for AI reading and its count.
  • Anthropic (USA): AI reading, only with your permission.
  • Resend (USA): e-mails with sign-in codes and links.
  • RevenueCat (USA): purchases of Pro.
  • Sentry (EU): crash reports without personal data.
  • Expo (USA): app updates.
  • Apple, Google and Meta: the App Store, Google Play and signing in with them, when you choose to.

Where data goes to the USA, the providers use the EU standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. We never sell data, and the app has no ads and no tracking.

Legal basis

  • To provide the app you use (GDPR article 6(1)(b)).
  • Your consent for AI reading (article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Our legitimate interest in fixing errors and keeping the service within its limits (article 6(1)(f)).

How long data is kept

  • On the phone: until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Your account and synced data: until you delete the account in the app.
  • Pictures and text for AI reading: not stored by us.
  • CSV and Excel files you import: not stored. Only the entries you save and the layout of the file’s columns remain.
  • Count of AI readings: 30 days.
  • Count of free AI scans (hashed device ID, month and number): 13 months.
  • Log of sign-in e-mails (no address): 7 days.
  • Crash reports: at most 90 days.

Your rights

You can see, correct and export all your data in the app (Me → Export data, free for everyone), and delete it (Me → Delete all data). You can object to processing and withdraw your consent for AI reading at any time.

If you have questions, write to kontakt@nordicdigitalapp.no. The supervisory authority is the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet), and you can complain to it. If you live in the EU/EEA, you can also complain to the data protection authority in your own country.

Children

Saldo Pocket is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly process their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top. If the change matters for how your data is used, we tell you in the app.
