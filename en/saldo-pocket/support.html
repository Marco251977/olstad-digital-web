Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

Both have the budget, the forecast, import from your bank and export of all your data. Pro has higher limits, all history (Free shows this period and the one before), reports to Excel, CSV and PDF, accrued interest, adjusting the forecast and sync between devices.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Pro is tied to your Apple ID or Google account. Sign in with the same account on the new phone, open Saldo Pocket and tap Restore purchases. Pro purchases are not open yet; this is how it will work.

How do I export my data?

Go to Me → Export data. Export all my data (JSON or CSV) is free for everyone. With Pro you can also export reports for a period and an account to Excel or CSV.

How do I import from my online bank?

Tap + and Import from online bank. Use screenshots or photos of the list, or a PDF, CSV or Excel file you have exported from your bank. You review every row before anything is saved, and rows you already have are marked. Saldo Pocket never connects to your bank.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Me → Delete all data. Everything on the phone is deleted. Read more on the page about deleting your data.
